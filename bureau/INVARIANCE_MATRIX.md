# Purpose

The Invariance Matrix identifies the properties of the PADI Sovereign Bureau that shall remain invariant over time.

An invariant is a constitutional property that shall not change unless the Constitution itself is amended.

Technologies may evolve.

Repositories may be reorganized.

Engineering methodologies may mature.

These invariants preserve the institutional identity of the Bureau.
