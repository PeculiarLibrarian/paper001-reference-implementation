# Introduction

The Practice Area Depth Index (PADI) is the official engineering methodology of the PADI Sovereign Bureau.

PADI provides a deterministic framework for engineering semantic infrastructures that are explainable, interoperable, governed, and institutionally sustainable.

PADI does not constitute the Bureau.

Rather, it is the engineering methodology through which the Bureau operationalizes the principles of Semantic Interoperability Engineering.

---

# Constitutional Status

Under the Constitution of the PADI Sovereign Bureau:

- Information Science is the intellectual foundation.
- Semantic Interoperability Engineering is the research discipline.
- PADI is the engineering methodology.

All engineering artifacts developed under Bureau authority shall conform to PADI.

---

# Purpose

PADI exists to transform theoretical principles into operational semantic systems.

Its objectives are to:

- standardize semantic engineering;
- promote deterministic system design;
- preserve semantic integrity;
- strengthen knowledge governance;
- support explainable organizational intelligence.

---

# Engineering Philosophy

PADI adopts the following engineering principles.

1. Meaning before computation.
2. Architecture before implementation.
3. Determinism before prediction.
4. Governance before automation.
5. Knowledge before intelligence.

These principles govern all PADI-compliant engineering.

---

# Scope

PADI governs the engineering of:

- Ontologies
- Knowledge Graphs
- Semantic Models
- Canonical Data Models
- Knowledge Governance
- Semantic Runtime Systems
- Explainable Inference Pipelines

PADI does not prescribe organizational policy, research agendas, or institutional governance.

These remain the responsibility of the Bureau.

---

# Practice Areas

PADI organizes engineering into coherent practice areas.

Typical practice areas include:

- Ontology Engineering
- Knowledge Representation
- Knowledge Graph Engineering
- Semantic Governance
- RDF Engineering
- SHACL Validation
- Semantic Runtime Engineering
- Institutional Knowledge Engineering

Each practice area shall define measurable engineering depth.

---

# Engineering Lifecycle

A PADI-compliant engineering process shall include:

1. Conceptual modelling.
2. Semantic modelling.
3. Canonical representation.
4. Validation.
5. Materialization.
6. Governance.
7. Operational deployment.
8. Continuous verification.

Every stage shall preserve semantic consistency.

---

# Compliance

Engineering shall be considered PADI-compliant only when it:

- preserves semantic meaning;
- maintains deterministic behaviour;
- employs governed knowledge structures;
- supports explainability;
- conforms to constitutional principles.

Compliance is an engineering property rather than a software feature.

---

# Relationship to Research

PADI is informed by the research programme of the PADI Sovereign Bureau.

Research generates theory.

PADI operationalizes theory.

Engineering validates theory.

Experience informs subsequent research.

This establishes a continuous cycle between scholarship and engineering.

---

# Long-Term Objective

The long-term objective of PADI is to provide a mature engineering methodology for Semantic Interoperability Engineering analogous to the role of software engineering methodologies within Computer Science.

PADI seeks to enable repeatable, governed, and explainable semantic engineering.

---

# Closing Statement

PADI is not a software product.

It is not a programming framework.

It is not an application.

It is an engineering methodology for building trustworthy semantic infrastructures.
