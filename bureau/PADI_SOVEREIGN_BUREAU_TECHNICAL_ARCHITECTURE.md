# Purpose

This document defines the constitutional technical architecture of the PADI Sovereign Bureau.

The architecture exists to operationalize the Bureau's research programme while preserving constitutional separation between theory, methodology, implementation, and institutional governance.

---

# Architectural Philosophy

The Bureau adopts a layered architecture.

Each architectural layer possesses a single constitutional responsibility.

Lower layers support higher layers.

Higher layers shall not redefine lower layers.

---

# Governance

Architectural decisions shall satisfy four requirements.

They shall:

- preserve constitutional boundaries;
- strengthen semantic integrity;
- reduce unnecessary complexity;
- improve institutional explainability.

Architecture shall never evolve through convenience alone.

---

# Architectural Invariants

The following invariants are constitutionally protected.

1. Information Science remains the intellectual foundation.

2. Semantic Interoperability Engineering remains the research discipline.

3. PADI remains the engineering methodology.

4. Peculiar Library remains the execution platform.

5. Peculiar Librarian remains the research programme.

Violation of these invariants constitutes architectural non-compliance.

---

# Long-Term Vision

The Bureau architecture is designed to outlive individual technologies.

Programming languages may change.

Frameworks may change.

Repositories may evolve.

The constitutional architecture shall remain stable.

---

# Closing Statement

The architecture of the PADI Sovereign Bureau is intentionally institutional rather than technological.

It exists to preserve meaning, strengthen governance, and advance Semantic Interoperability Engineering through disciplined engineering practice.
