# Migration to PADI v1.0
## Constitutional Engineering Migration Framework

PADI Sovereign Bureau

Version 1.0

---

# Purpose

This document defines the official migration process through which an existing engineering project becomes compliant with the Practice Area Depth Index (PADI).

Migration is a constitutional engineering activity.

Its objective is not merely software modernization, but alignment with the engineering methodology of the PADI Sovereign Bureau.

---

# Constitutional Context

Under the Constitution of the PADI Sovereign Bureau:

- Information Science provides the intellectual foundation.
- Semantic Interoperability Engineering provides the research discipline.
- PADI provides the engineering methodology.

Migration therefore consists of aligning engineering practice with constitutional principles.

---

# Migration Objectives

Every migration shall seek to:

- improve semantic clarity;
- strengthen knowledge governance;
- establish deterministic behaviour;
- preserve institutional meaning;
- increase interoperability;
- improve explainability.

Migration shall not be undertaken solely for technological novelty.

---

# Migration Principles

Every migration shall preserve the following constitutional principles.

1. Meaning shall not be lost.

2. Knowledge shall remain traceable.

3. Architecture shall precede implementation.

4. Governance shall remain explicit.

5. Deterministic behaviour shall be preserved.

---

# Migration Stages

## Stage 1

Conceptual Assessment

Determine:

- institutional purpose;
- knowledge domain;
- semantic boundaries.

---

## Stage 2

Semantic Assessment

Evaluate:

- terminology;
- ontology;
- metadata;
- conceptual consistency.

---

## Stage 3

Architectural Alignment

Align the system with PADI engineering practice.

Separate:

- research;
- methodology;
- implementation;
- governance.

---

## Stage 4

Canonical Representation

Represent institutional knowledge through governed semantic models.

Canonical representation becomes the authoritative organizational representation.

---

## Stage 5

Validation

Engineering shall be validated through:

- semantic consistency;
- deterministic behaviour;
- governance compliance;
- constitutional alignment.

---

## Stage 6

Operational Integration

The migrated system becomes part of a governed semantic infrastructure.

Migration concludes only when constitutional compliance is demonstrated.

---

# Compliance Criteria

Migration shall be considered complete only when the resulting system:

- conforms to PADI;
- preserves institutional meaning;
- supports deterministic reasoning;
- maintains semantic interoperability;
- satisfies constitutional governance.

---

# Non-Goals

Migration shall not pursue:

- unnecessary architectural complexity;
- technology replacement without semantic benefit;
- implementation-driven redesign;
- loss of institutional knowledge.

Engineering serves knowledge.

Knowledge does not serve engineering.

---

# Continuous Improvement

Migration represents the beginning rather than the conclusion of engineering governance.

Every migrated system shall remain subject to:

- constitutional review;
- semantic review;
- engineering verification;
- architectural refinement.

---

# Closing Statement

Migration to PADI is fundamentally a migration toward disciplined semantic engineering.

Its success is measured not by software change, but by improved organizational knowledge.
