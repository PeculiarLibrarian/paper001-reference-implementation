# Purpose

This Research Agenda establishes the long-term scholarly priorities of the PADI Sovereign Bureau.

It guides the Bureau's research programme, engineering activities, publications, and institutional collaborations.

The agenda is intended to remain stable over multiple years while allowing individual research projects to evolve.

---

# Mission

To advance Semantic Interoperability Engineering as an academic and engineering discipline grounded in the principles of Information Science.

---

# Strategic Research Theme I
## Foundations of Semantic Interoperability Engineering

- Foundational theory

- Conceptual models

# Research Principles

Every Bureau research project shall:

- strengthen Information Science;
- advance Semantic Interoperability Engineering;
- preserve semantic integrity;
- contribute reproducible knowledge;
- demonstrate institutional relevance.

Research that does not satisfy these principles shall not become part of the official programme.

---

# Expected Long-Term Outcomes

The Bureau seeks to establish:

- Semantic Interoperability Engineering as a recognized discipline;
- PADI as a mature engineering methodology;
- reference semantic architectures for institutions;
- internationally relevant scholarly publications;
- enduring contributions to Information Science.

---

# Closing Statement

The Bureau's research agenda reflects a commitment to the systematic advancement of knowledge rather than the pursuit of short-term technological trends.

Its success will be measured by the enduring value of its contributions to scholarship, engineering, and institutional practice.
