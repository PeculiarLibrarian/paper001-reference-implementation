# Introduction

The PADI Sovereign Bureau conducts research within the intellectual tradition of Information Science.

Its research is directed toward the advancement of Semantic Interoperability Engineering as an academic and engineering discipline.

The Bureau regards research as the systematic pursuit of understanding rather than the production of software.

Software is an outcome of research.

It is not the purpose of research.

---

# Philosophical Position

The Bureau adopts the position that organizational intelligence depends fundamentally upon organized knowledge.

Meaning precedes reasoning.

Knowledge precedes intelligence.

Governance precedes automation.

Accordingly, research begins with understanding the organization of knowledge before attempting to automate its use.

---

# Intellectual Foundation

The Bureau recognizes Information Science as its primary intellectual foundation.

Its work draws upon the long-established traditions of:

- Knowledge Organization
- Classification
- Metadata
- Authority Control
- Information Retrieval
- Information Governance
- Preservation

Semantic technologies extend these traditions rather than replace them.

---

# Research Orientation

Research conducted by the Bureau shall be:

- theory-informed;
- evidence-based;
- architecturally coherent;
- reproducible;
- explainable;
- institutionally relevant.

Research shall contribute to enduring knowledge rather than transient technological trends.

---

# Engineering as Research

The Bureau recognizes engineering as a legitimate form of scholarly inquiry when it advances theoretical understanding.

Engineering artifacts therefore serve as:

- research instruments;
- experimental platforms;
- demonstrators of theory.

Their purpose is to validate knowledge rather than merely deliver functionality.

---

# View of Technology

Technology is regarded as temporary.

Knowledge is regarded as enduring.

Programming languages, frameworks, and software platforms evolve.

Well-founded principles of knowledge organization persist.

Research shall therefore prioritize principles over implementations.

---

# Scientific Method

Research shall proceed through:

1. Observation of institutional problems.
2. Conceptual analysis.
3. Theoretical formulation.
4. Semantic modelling.
5. Engineering implementation.
6. Validation.
7. Reflection.
8. Publication.

Each stage informs the next in a continuous cycle of scientific inquiry.

---

# Standards of Evidence

Official Bureau research shall distinguish clearly between:

- observation;
- interpretation;
- evidence;
- theory;
- engineering;
- opinion.

Claims shall remain proportionate to available evidence.

Engineering success alone shall not constitute scientific proof.

---

# Long-Term Perspective

The Bureau measures success by its contribution to the enduring body of Information Science.

Its objective is not the creation of isolated software systems.

Its objective is the advancement of methods through which institutions organize, govern, preserve, and exchange knowledge.

---

# Closing Statement

The Bureau believes that the future of intelligent organizations will depend less upon increasingly sophisticated algorithms than upon increasingly well-organized knowledge.

Information Science provides the intellectual foundation.

Semantic Interoperability Engineering extends that foundation into computational environments.

The Bureau exists to contribute to that evolution through disciplined research.
