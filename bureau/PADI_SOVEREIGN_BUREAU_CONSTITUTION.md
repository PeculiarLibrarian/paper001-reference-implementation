# CONSTITUTION
## PADI Sovereign Bureau

Version 2.0.0

Ratified: 2026

---

# PREAMBLE

The PADI Sovereign Bureau is constituted as an independent research institution dedicated to advancing Semantic Interoperability Engineering as an academic and engineering discipline founded upon Information Science.

This Constitution is the supreme governing authority of the Bureau.

Every doctrine, framework, specification, architecture, repository, methodology, publication, software system, and research programme operating under the authority of the Bureau shall conform to this Constitution.

---

# ARTICLE I
## Constitutional Authority

This Constitution is the supreme governing document of the PADI Sovereign Bureau.

No Bureau document shall supersede this Constitution.

Any inconsistency shall be resolved in favour of this Constitution.

---

# ARTICLE II
## Institutional Mission

The Bureau shall advance knowledge through:

- Research
- Engineering
- Publication
- Education
- Institutional collaboration

The Bureau shall pursue these activities exclusively in support of Semantic Interoperability Engineering.

---

# ARTICLE III
## Intellectual Foundation

The Bureau formally recognizes Information Science as its intellectual foundation.

All Bureau research shall remain consistent with the principles of:

- Knowledge Organization
- Classification
- Metadata
- Authority Control
- Information Governance
- Preservation

The Bureau shall not redefine these foundations without constitutional amendment.

---

# ARTICLE IV
## Research Discipline

The Bureau recognizes Semantic Interoperability Engineering as its principal field of research.

Research conducted under Bureau authority shall contribute to one or more of the following:

- Theory
- Methodology
- Engineering
- Governance
- Education

Research unrelated to Semantic Interoperability Engineering shall not constitute official Bureau work.

---

# ARTICLE V
## Engineering Methodology

The Practice Area Depth Index (PADI) is the official engineering methodology of the Bureau.

Every Bureau engineering artifact shall be demonstrably compatible with PADI.

Alternative methodologies may be investigated for research purposes but shall not replace PADI without constitutional amendment.

---

# ARTICLE VI
## Research Integrity

Every official Bureau publication shall satisfy the following requirements.

It shall:

- identify its purpose;
- identify its theoretical basis;
- define terminology explicitly;
- distinguish evidence from opinion;
- preserve provenance where applicable;
- remain internally consistent.

Research that fails these requirements shall not receive constitutional recognition.

---

# ARTICLE VII
## Semantic Governance

The Bureau shall engineer knowledge before engineering intelligence.

Accordingly, every semantic system shall prioritize:

- explicit meaning;
- deterministic interpretation;
- semantic governance;
- explainability;
- interoperability.

No system shall intentionally sacrifice semantic integrity for computational convenience.

---

# ARTICLE VIII
## Open Standards

Official Bureau engineering shall preferentially adopt internationally recognized semantic standards including:

- RDF
- RDFS
- OWL
- SKOS
- SHACL
- SPARQL
- JSON-LD

Proprietary technologies may be employed provided they do not compromise interoperability.

---

# ARTICLE IX
## Constitutional Principles

Every Bureau activity shall conform to the following constitutional principles.

1. Meaning precedes computation.

2. Evidence precedes assertion.

3. Governance precedes automation.

4. Determinism precedes prediction.

5. Architecture precedes implementation.

6. Knowledge shall be preserved as institutional infrastructure.

These principles are constitutionally binding.

---

# ARTICLE X
## Bureau Publications

Official Bureau publications shall contribute to at least one of the following.

- Research
- Engineering
- Governance
- Education
- Institutional Practice

Documents that do not contribute to the Bureau's mission shall not become constitutional artifacts.

---

# ARTICLE XI
## Repository Governance

Official repositories shall remain organized according to constitutional boundaries.

The Bureau recognizes three primary systems.

- PADI
- Peculiar Library
- Peculiar Librarian

No repository shall expand beyond its constitutional purpose.

Repository organization shall prioritize clarity over convenience.

---

# ARTICLE XII
## Amendment Procedure

This Constitution may be amended only when an amendment:

- strengthens constitutional coherence;
- advances the Bureau's mission;
- preserves institutional continuity;
- remains consistent with Information Science.

Every amendment shall supersede previous constitutional language while preserving the historical record.

---

# ARTICLE XIII
## Constitutional Compliance

Every Bureau artifact shall be considered constitutionally compliant only when it:

- supports the Bureau's mission;
- aligns with Information Science;
- advances Semantic Interoperability Engineering;
- conforms to PADI where engineering is involved;
- remains consistent with this Constitution.

Constitutional compliance shall take precedence over implementation convenience.

---

# ARTICLE XIV
## Institutional Continuity

The Bureau exists independently of any single software implementation.

Programming languages may evolve.

Frameworks may disappear.

Repositories may be reorganized.

The constitutional mission of the Bureau shall remain unchanged.

---

# RATIFICATION

This Constitution is hereby adopted as the supreme governing authority of the PADI Sovereign Bureau.

All present and future Bureau activities shall be interpreted according to its provisions.
