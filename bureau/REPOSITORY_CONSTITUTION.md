# REPOSITORY CONSTITUTION
## PADI Sovereign Bureau

Version 2.0.0

Constitutionally subordinate to:
**PADI Sovereign Bureau Constitution**

---

# Purpose

This Constitution establishes the constitutional organization and governance of all repositories operating under the authority of the PADI Sovereign Bureau.

Its purpose is to preserve architectural integrity by assigning a single constitutional responsibility to every repository.

Repository expansion beyond constitutional scope is prohibited.

---

# Constitutional Principle

One repository.

One constitutional purpose.

Every repository shall possess exactly one primary responsibility.

Cross-repository coupling shall remain minimal.

---

# Constitutional Repository Hierarchy

The Bureau recognizes three constitutional systems.

## I. PADI

Constitutional Role:

Engineering Methodology

Purpose:

To define the engineering methodology through which Semantic Interoperability Engineering is implemented.

Responsibilities include:

- Engineering standards
- Semantic validation methodology
- Practice Area Depth Index
- Engineering specifications
- Compliance methodology

PADI shall not become an application.

PADI shall not become a research archive.

---

## II. Peculiar Library

Constitutional Role:

Semantic Execution Platform

Purpose:

To implement semantic infrastructures defined by the Bureau.

Responsibilities include:

- Ontology Engineering
- Knowledge Graph Engineering
- Semantic Runtime
- Inference
- Knowledge Governance
- Semantic Management Information Systems
- RDF infrastructure
- SHACL validation
- SPARQL execution

Peculiar Library shall remain implementation-oriented.

Research publications shall not originate here.

---

## III. Peculiar Librarian

Constitutional Role:

Research Programme

Purpose:

To conduct and publish research advancing Semantic Interoperability Engineering.

Responsibilities include:

- Research
- Publications
- Bureau constitutions
- White papers
- Engineering doctrine
- Research philosophy
- Research agenda
- Theory development

Software implementation shall remain secondary.

The repository exists primarily to advance scholarship.

---

# Repository Independence

Each repository shall remain independently maintainable.

No repository shall require another repository merely to preserve its constitutional identity.

Integration is permitted.

Dependency is discouraged.

---

# Constitutional Boundaries

The following transfers are prohibited.

Research publications into Peculiar Library.

Runtime implementation into Peculiar Librarian.

Engineering methodology into implementation repositories.

Each repository shall preserve constitutional separation of concerns.

---

# Repository Growth

Repository growth shall satisfy three constitutional tests.

## Necessity

Does the artifact belong constitutionally?

## Cohesion

Does it strengthen repository identity?

## Simplicity

Does it reduce rather than increase architectural complexity?

Failure of any test constitutes constitutional non-compliance.

---

# Bureau Governance

The repositories collectively implement the mission of the Bureau.

Information Science provides the intellectual foundation.

Semantic Interoperability Engineering provides the research discipline.

PADI provides the engineering methodology.

Peculiar Library provides execution.

Peculiar Librarian provides scholarship.

Each repository therefore serves one constitutional responsibility.

---

# Constitutional Compliance

Every repository shall remain auditable against this Constitution.

Architectural convenience shall never supersede constitutional integrity.

---

# Ratification

This Repository Constitution is adopted under the authority of the PADI Sovereign Bureau Constitution.

All repository organization shall conform to its provisions.
