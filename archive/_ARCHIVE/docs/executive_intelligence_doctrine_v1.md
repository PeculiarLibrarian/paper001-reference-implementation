# Executive Intelligence Doctrine
Version 1.0.0

---

# Purpose

This doctrine defines Executive Intelligence within the Peculiar Ecosystem.

Executive Intelligence is not information.

Executive Intelligence is not analytics.

Executive Intelligence is not artificial intelligence.

Executive Intelligence is institutional understanding derived through
governed semantic reasoning.

---

# Definition

Executive Intelligence is evidence-based strategic understanding that enables
senior decision-makers to allocate resources, manage risk, govern institutions
and create long-term value.

# Executive Questions

Every intelligence product shall answer:

What happened?

Why did it happen?

Why does it matter?

What changes if nothing is done?

What opportunities exist?

What risks exist?

What trade-offs exist?

What should executives consider?

# Constitutional Limitation

The Peculiar Librarian does not make executive decisions.

It provides the highest attainable institutional intelligence in support of
executive judgement.

Decision authority always remains with human executives.
