# First Principles of the Peculiar Ecosystem
Version 1.0.0

---

# Purpose

This document establishes the irreducible first principles upon which the
Peculiar Ecosystem is founded.

Every constitutional document derives from these principles.

Every implementation SHALL preserve them.

---

# First Principle

Knowledge precedes intelligence.

Without knowledge there is no intelligence.

---

# Second Principle

Meaning precedes computation.

Software manipulates symbols.

The Library preserves meaning.

---

# Third Principle

Evidence precedes recommendation.

Recommendations derive from evidence.

Evidence does not derive from recommendations.

---

# Fourth Principle

Reasoning precedes judgment.

Judgment shall always emerge from governed reasoning.

---

# Fifth Principle

Context creates meaning.

Knowledge removed from context loses institutional value.

---

# Sixth Principle

Relationships create understanding.

Isolated facts possess limited value.

Linked knowledge produces institutional understanding.

---

# Seventh Principle

Institutions outlive implementations.

Technology evolves.

Institutions preserve continuity.

---

# Eighth Principle

Semantic interoperability preserves institutional memory.

Knowledge shall remain understandable across:

systems

vendors

programming languages

reasoning engines

time

---

# Ninth Principle

Professional judgment remains human.

The Peculiar Librarian augments executive judgment.

It never replaces it.

---

# Tenth Principle

Truth is constitutional.

Convenience is operational.

Operational convenience shall never override constitutional truth.

---

# Eleventh Principle

Every conclusion requires provenance.

No constitutional exception exists.

---

# Twelfth Principle

Knowledge compounds.

Institutional knowledge is cumulative.

Every validated engagement strengthens future reasoning.

---

# Thirteenth Principle

Practice Areas define professional depth.

Breadth without depth does not produce executive intelligence.

---

# Fourteenth Principle

Capital follows understanding.

Superior institutional understanding enables superior capital allocation.

---

# Fifteenth Principle

The purpose of intelligence is better decisions.

Intelligence is valuable only insofar as it improves executive decision quality.

---

# Constitutional Observation

The Peculiar Ecosystem is not fundamentally a software platform.

It is an institutional knowledge system whose current implementation happens
to be software.
