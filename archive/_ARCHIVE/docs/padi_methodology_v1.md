# PADI Methodology v1.0.0

## Purpose

PADI is the governing methodology for executive intelligence within the
Peculiar ecosystem.

PADI has two complementary meanings.

- Practice Area Depth Index
- Practice Area Depth Inference

The Index measures analytical depth.

The Inference governs semantic reasoning across that depth.

Together they define how institutional knowledge is transformed into
executive intelligence.

---

# Mission

To derive evidence-based strategic intelligence from validated institutional
knowledge through semantic reasoning.

---

# Vision

To establish a digital librarian capable of producing executive-quality
strategic intelligence that supports board members, executives, investors,
regulators and professional advisors.

---

# Philosophy

Knowledge precedes intelligence.

Evidence precedes conclusions.

Reasoning precedes recommendations.

Recommendations precede decisions.

No executive judgment shall exist without traceable evidence.

## Depth Index

The Practice Area Depth Index measures analytical maturity.

Typical progression:

Level 1

Facts

↓

Level 2

Relationships

↓

Level 3

Patterns

↓

Level 4

Strategic Implications

↓

Level 5

Executive Judgment

↓

Level 6

Scenario Analysis

↓

Level 7

Strategic Recommendation

Each engagement explicitly declares the depth reached.

---

## Depth Inference

Practice Area Depth Inference governs reasoning.

Reasoning SHALL progress only through validated evidence.

Inference SHALL remain within declared practice area boundaries.

Cross-domain inference SHALL explicitly document assumptions.

2. Semantic Integrity

3. MECE Analysis

4. Materiality

5. Explainability

6. Provenance

7. Reproducibility

---

# Relationship to Peculiar Library

Peculiar Library provides institutional knowledge.

PADI provides the reasoning methodology.

Peculiar Librarian executes the methodology.

Together they produce executive intelligence.

---

# Relationship to Artificial Intelligence

Artificial Intelligence is an assisting capability.

Artificial Intelligence is not the authoritative source of truth.

Validated institutional knowledge remains authoritative.

All executive outputs SHALL remain reproducible from semantic knowledge.
