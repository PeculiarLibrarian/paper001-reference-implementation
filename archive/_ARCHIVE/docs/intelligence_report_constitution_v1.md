# Intelligence Report Constitution
Version 1.0.0

---

# Purpose

This constitution defines the mandatory structure of every intelligence report
produced by the Peculiar Librarian.

Reports are governed reasoning products.

They are not narratives.

They are not essays.

They are not summaries.

Every section shall be supported by semantic evidence.

---

# Constitutional Principles

Every report shall be:

Objective

Evidence-based

Traceable

Auditable

Explainable

Reproducible

Material

Decision-oriented

Executive-ready

---

# Mandatory Structure

Every report shall contain the following sections.

1.
Executive Summary

2.
Institution Overview

3.
Strategic Context

4.
Evidence Summary

5.
Semantic Findings

6.
Cross-Domain Analysis

7.
Material Risks

8.
Material Opportunities

9.
Strategic Alternatives

10.
Scenario Analysis

11.
Executive Implications

12.
Investment Perspective

13.
Confidence Assessment

14.
Evidence Trace

15.
Appendices

No section may be omitted.

---

# Executive Summary

Must answer:

What matters?

Why now?

Why should executives care?

---

# Institution Overview

Describe:

Business

Structure

Industry

Operating environment

---

# Strategic Context

Describe:

Macroeconomic context

Industry context

Regulatory context

Competitive context

---

# Evidence Summary

Summarize validated evidence only.

No interpretation.

---

# Semantic Findings

Present findings generated through governed reasoning.

Every finding shall reference evidence.

---

# Cross-Domain Analysis

Every report shall integrate multiple domains where applicable.

Examples include:

Finance

Governance

Organization

Telecommunications

Legal

Regulatory

Risk

---

# Material Risks

Every identified risk shall include:

Description

Likelihood

Potential impact

Supporting evidence

---

# Material Opportunities

Every opportunity shall include:

Description

Strategic significance

Supporting evidence

---

# Strategic Alternatives

Describe possible executive responses.

Do not recommend without supporting evidence.

---

# Scenario Analysis

Every report shall evaluate:

Best case

Expected case

Worst case

---

# Executive Implications

Describe implications for:

Board

Executive Management

Investors

Regulators

Stakeholders

---

# Investment Perspective

Assess implications for capital allocation.

The Peculiar Librarian shall support investment judgement.

It shall not provide investment advice.

---

# Confidence Assessment

Every conclusion shall include an explicit confidence level derived from:

Evidence completeness

Semantic consistency

Cross-domain agreement

Reasoning quality

---

# Evidence Trace

Every major conclusion shall be traceable to:

Source evidence

Semantic entities

Reasoning chain

Graph artifacts

Ledger entry

---

# Constitutional Requirement

No report may contain unsupported conclusions.

No recommendation may exist without evidence.

No interpretation may violate semantic reasoning.
