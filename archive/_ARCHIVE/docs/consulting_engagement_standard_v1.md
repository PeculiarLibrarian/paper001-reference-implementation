# Consulting Engagement Standard v1.0.0

## Purpose

This standard defines the lifecycle of every executive consulting engagement
performed by the Peculiar Librarian.

An engagement is the highest-level unit of executive work.

All operators, semantic reasoning, and executive reports SHALL belong to an
engagement.

---

# Definition

An engagement is a structured investigation performed to answer a strategic
question using validated institutional knowledge governed by PADI.

---

# Guiding Principles

Every engagement SHALL be:

• Evidence-based

• Traceable

• Reproducible

• MECE

• Material

• Explainable

• Practice-area governed

---

# Engagement Lifecycle

Stage 1

Client Question

The engagement begins with a clearly defined executive question.

Examples

"What are Safaricom's strategic risks over the next five years?"

"Should an investor increase exposure?"

"What governance weaknesses require board attention?"

No reasoning begins before the question is defined.

---

Stage 2

Scope Definition

Define:

• organisation

• reporting period

• geography

• practice areas

• assumptions

• constraints

---

Stage 3

Practice Area Selection

Applicable PADI practice areas SHALL be declared.

Examples

Corporate Strategy

Corporate Finance

Governance

Telecommunications

Competition

Risk

Legal

ESG

---

Stage 4

Knowledge Acquisition

Retrieve validated knowledge from the Peculiar Library.

Sources SHALL remain immutable.

No executive judgment occurs during acquisition.

---

Stage 5

Semantic Compilation

Knowledge SHALL be transformed into a canonical semantic graph.

Compilation SHALL preserve provenance.

---

Stage 6

Reasoning

Reasoning SHALL operate only upon validated semantic knowledge.

Cross-domain inference SHALL document assumptions.

---

Stage 7

Evidence Synthesis

Evidence SHALL be organised into executive findings.

Each finding SHALL include:

• supporting evidence

• provenance

• confidence

• materiality

---

Stage 8

Strategic Analysis

Strategic analysis SHALL identify:

• strengths

• weaknesses

• opportunities

• threats

• dependencies

• constraints

• strategic drivers

• emerging patterns

---

Stage 9

Recommendations

Recommendations SHALL include:

expected benefit

associated risks

trade-offs

required assumptions

implementation dependencies

Recommendations SHALL never exist without evidence.

---

Stage 10

Executive Report

The final deliverable SHALL contain:

Executive Summary

Context

Methodology

Findings

Strategic Risks

Strategic Opportunities

Scenario Analysis

Recommendations

Confidence Assessment

Evidence Appendix

Provenance Appendix

---

# Engagement Deliverables

Every engagement SHALL produce:

Semantic Graph

Execution Ledger

Evidence Register

Executive Findings

Recommendations

Executive Report

---

# Quality Standards

Every engagement SHALL satisfy:

Evidence Completeness

Semantic Consistency

MECE Analysis

Materiality Assessment

Confidence Assessment

Reproducibility

Provenance Verification

---

# Success Criteria

An engagement is complete only when every executive statement can be traced
through the following chain.

Executive Statement

↓

Recommendation

↓

Finding

↓

Evidence

↓

Knowledge Graph

↓

Source Document

↓

Materialization Ledger
