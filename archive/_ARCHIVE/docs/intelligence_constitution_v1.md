# Intelligence Constitution v1.0.0

## Purpose

This Constitution establishes the governing principles for intelligence
produced by the Peculiar ecosystem.

It defines the nature, origin, validation and lifecycle of intelligence.

This Constitution is authoritative.

---

# Definition

Within the Peculiar ecosystem, intelligence is defined as:

Evidence-based strategic understanding derived from validated institutional
knowledge through semantic reasoning governed by PADI.

Intelligence is never synonymous with information.

Information becomes intelligence only after semantic validation, reasoning and
executive interpretation.

---

# Principle 1

Knowledge Precedes Intelligence

No intelligence SHALL exist without validated institutional knowledge.

---

# Principle 2

Evidence Governs Intelligence

Every intelligence product SHALL resolve to supporting evidence.

Unsupported intelligence SHALL be considered invalid.

---

# Principle 3

Reasoning Governs Inference

Inference SHALL occur only through documented semantic reasoning.

Inference SHALL remain reproducible.

---

# Principle 4

Judgment Requires Context

Executive judgment SHALL consider:

practice area

materiality

constraints

dependencies

strategic context

time horizon

---

# Principle 5

Recommendations Are Not Intelligence

Recommendations are executive actions informed by intelligence.

They SHALL remain distinct from findings.

---

# Principle 6

Artificial Intelligence

Artificial Intelligence is an assisting capability.

Artificial Intelligence SHALL NOT become the authoritative source of truth.

Institutional knowledge remains authoritative.

---

# Principle 7

Semantic Integrity

All intelligence SHALL preserve:

semantic consistency

logical consistency

provenance

reproducibility

---

# Principle 8

Executive Materiality

Only materially significant intelligence SHALL appear within executive
summaries.

Operational observations SHALL remain supporting evidence.
