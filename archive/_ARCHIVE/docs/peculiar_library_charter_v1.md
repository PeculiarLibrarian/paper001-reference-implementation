# Peculiar Library Charter v1.0.0

## Preamble

The Peculiar Library is an institutional semantic knowledge repository
established to preserve, organise, validate and govern knowledge for
executive decision support.

It exists to ensure that strategic intelligence is derived from validated
institutional knowledge rather than speculation.

The Library is governed by constitutional principles rather than individual
implementations.

---

# Mission

To organise institutional knowledge into a semantically interoperable
repository capable of supporting evidence-based executive reasoning.

---

# Vision

To become a globally trusted institutional knowledge system for executive
decision support across finance, governance, telecommunications, law,
strategy and other professional practice areas.

---

# Core Purpose

The Library SHALL:

preserve knowledge

organise knowledge

validate knowledge

govern knowledge

enable semantic interoperability

support executive intelligence

protect provenance

maintain institutional memory

---

# Institutional Responsibilities

The Library SHALL remain:

accurate

traceable

reproducible

auditable

vendor-neutral

technology-neutral

semantically consistent

constitutionally governed

---

# Knowledge Stewardship

Knowledge entrusted to the Library SHALL retain:

meaning

context

provenance

relationships

validation status

history

semantic identity

---

# Institutional Memory

The Library serves as the permanent institutional memory of every engagement.

Knowledge SHALL outlive:

operators

implementations

software versions

reasoning engines

language models

technology stacks

---

# Semantic Interoperability

Semantic interoperability SHALL remain a constitutional requirement.

Knowledge SHALL be represented using open standards whenever practical.

Institutional meaning SHALL never depend upon proprietary software.

---

# Executive Responsibility

The Library SHALL support executive reasoning without replacing executive
judgment.

It provides evidence.

It does not make decisions.

---

# Relationship to PADI

PADI governs reasoning.

The Library governs knowledge.

The Librarian applies PADI to knowledge.

Together they derive executive intelligence.

---

# Relationship to the Peculiar Librarian

The Peculiar Librarian is the operating intelligence of the Library.

The Library stores institutional knowledge.

The Librarian derives institutional intelligence.

Neither replaces the other.

---

# Constitutional Commitment

The Peculiar Library is committed to:

truth before convenience

evidence before opinion

knowledge before intelligence

reasoning before recommendation

provenance before publication

institutional continuity before implementation

---

# Charter Principle

Technology may evolve.

Implementations may change.

Ontologies may expand.

Practice Areas may grow.

The constitutional responsibilities of the Peculiar Library remain unchanged.
