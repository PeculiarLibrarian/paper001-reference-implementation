# Peculiar Consulting Canon v1.0.0

## Purpose

The Peculiar Consulting Canon establishes the permanent analytical principles
that govern every consulting engagement conducted by the Peculiar Librarian.

The Canon exists independently of technology, implementation and software.

It governs professional thinking.

---

# Principle 1

Understand Before Explaining

No recommendation shall be produced before the institution has been
understood.

---

# Principle 2

Evidence Before Opinion

Professional opinion has no constitutional authority.

Evidence possesses constitutional authority.

---

# Principle 3

Context Before Conclusion

Every observation exists within institutional context.

Removing context invalidates interpretation.

---

# Principle 4

Structure Before Analysis

Problems shall first be organised.

Only organised problems may be analysed.

---

# Principle 5

MECE

Every executive analysis shall be:

Mutually Exclusive.

Collectively Exhaustive.

Overlap shall be eliminated wherever practical.

---

# Principle 6

Materiality

Only materially significant observations belong within executive reporting.

Operational detail supports—not replaces—executive intelligence.

---

# Principle 7

Systems Thinking

Institutions shall be analysed as interconnected systems.

No entity exists in isolation.

Dependencies shall always be identified.

---

# Principle 8

Causality

Reports shall distinguish:

facts

relationships

correlations

causes

consequences

Recommendations shall never be based solely upon correlation.

---

# Principle 9

Strategic Time Horizons

Executive reasoning shall consider:

Immediate

Short-term

Medium-term

Long-term

Structural consequences.

---

# Principle 10

Trade-offs

Every recommendation creates trade-offs.

Trade-offs shall always be disclosed.

---

# Principle 11

Executive Simplicity

Complex reasoning shall produce simple executive communication.

Sophisticated analysis should never require sophisticated reading.

---

# Principle 12

Institutional Integrity

Consulting exists to improve institutional decision quality.

It does not exist to justify predetermined conclusions.

---

# Canonical Deliverable

Every consulting engagement shall improve one or more of the following:

Decision quality

Risk awareness

Strategic clarity

Institutional understanding

Capital allocation

Governance maturity

Operational resilience

Long-term value creation
