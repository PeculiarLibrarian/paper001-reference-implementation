# Peculiar Library Institutional Semantic Metamodel
Version: 1.0.0

---

# Purpose

The Institutional Semantic Metamodel defines the semantic architecture of every knowledge model implemented inside the Peculiar Library.

It specifies the universal semantic building blocks from which all Practice Areas are constructed.

This document is normative.

All domain ontologies SHALL conform to this metamodel.

---

# Layer 1 — Institution

Represents the real-world institutional environment.

Examples include:

- governments
- corporations
- regulators
- financial markets
- courts
- healthcare systems

Institution defines context.

---

# Layer 2 — Practice Area

A Practice Area represents a bounded body of institutional expertise.

Examples:

- Finance
- Organization
- Governance
- Telecommunications
- Legal
- Healthcare

Practice Areas organize knowledge.

---

# Layer 3 — Engagement

An Engagement represents an analytical mission.

Examples:

- Due diligence
- Investment assessment
- Regulatory review
- Strategic transformation
- Risk assessment
- Market intelligence

Every Engagement belongs to exactly one Practice Area.

---

# Layer 4 — Question

Questions define the objectives of an Engagement.

No Engagement exists without Questions.

Questions drive knowledge acquisition.

---

# Layer 5 — Evidence

Evidence represents verifiable institutional facts.

Evidence may originate from:

- financial statements
- legislation
- contracts
- annual reports
- interviews
- market data
- regulatory filings

Evidence never contains recommendations.

---

# Layer 6 — Finding

Findings are analytical conclusions derived from evidence.

Findings transform evidence into institutional knowledge.

---

# Layer 7 — Issue

Issues identify institutional conditions requiring attention.

Issues divide into:

- Risks
- Opportunities

---

# Layer 8 — Recommendation

Recommendations propose institutional action.

Every Recommendation shall be supported by Findings.

---

# Layer 9 — Decision

Decisions represent executive commitment.

Recommendations inform Decisions.

---

# Layer 10 — Outcome

Outcomes measure institutional consequences.

Outcomes become future evidence.

This creates a continuous institutional learning cycle.

---

# Layer 11 — Executive Intelligence

Executive Intelligence communicates institutional understanding.

Deliverables include:

- Executive reports
- Investment memoranda
- Strategy papers
- Risk reports
- Board briefings
- Regulatory assessments

Executive Intelligence is the terminal product of the Peculiar Library.

---

# Universal Semantic Objects

Every Practice Area SHALL define:

- Concepts
- Relationships
- Constraints
- Taxonomies
- Evidence
- Findings
- Recommendations
- Queries
- Reports

No Practice Area may omit these semantic primitives.

# Design Principles

The metamodel follows these principles:

1. Technology independence
2. Explainability
3. Semantic interoperability
4. Institutional traceability
5. Executive usability
6. Constitutional governance
7. Knowledge before computation
8. Evidence before recommendation
9. Recommendation before decision
10. Decision before outcome
