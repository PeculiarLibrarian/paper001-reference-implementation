# Institutional Architecture Principles
Version: 1.0.0

---

# Purpose

This document establishes the constitutional architecture principles governing the Peculiar Library.

Every component, ontology, taxonomy, validation rule, reasoning engine, execution pipeline, and executive report SHALL conform to these principles.

These principles are technology independent.

---

# Principle 1
Knowledge Before Computation

The primary asset of the Peculiar Library is institutional knowledge.

Software exists only to organize, validate, reason over, and communicate knowledge.

---

# Principle 2
Meaning Before Implementation

Every concept SHALL be defined conceptually before implementation.

Conceptual integrity precedes software implementation.

---

# Principle 3
Institution Before Technology

The institutional model governs the implementation.

Technology SHALL never redefine institutional meaning.

---

# Principle 4
Evidence Before Conclusions

Every conclusion shall originate from verifiable evidence.

No recommendation shall exist without supporting evidence.

---

# Principle 5
Traceability

Every executive conclusion shall be traceable through:

Recommendation

↓

Finding

↓

Evidence

↓

Source

Semantic provenance is mandatory.

---

# Principle 6
Explainability

Every inference produced by the library shall be explainable.

Opaque reasoning is prohibited.

---

# Principle 7
Semantic Interoperability

Knowledge shall be represented using internationally recognized semantic standards.

Implementation shall prioritize interoperability over proprietary representation.

---

# Principle 8
Separation of Concerns

The Peculiar Library distinguishes between:

Institution

Knowledge

Semantics

Execution

Presentation

Each layer has independent responsibilities.

---

# Principle 9
Practice Area Independence

Practice Areas extend the constitutional model.

Practice Areas shall never redefine constitutional concepts.

---

# Principle 10
Institutional Memory

Every completed engagement contributes reusable institutional knowledge.

Knowledge accumulation is cumulative.

---

# Principle 11
Executive Orientation

Every analytical process ultimately supports executive decision-making.

If a component cannot contribute to executive intelligence, it does not belong in the constitutional architecture.

---

# Principle 12
Continuous Learning

Institutional learning shall occur through:

new evidence

↓

new findings

↓

updated knowledge

↓

improved future reasoning

---

# Principle 13
Technology Neutrality

The constitutional architecture shall remain independent of:

Programming language

Database

Reasoner

Triple store

Cloud provider

User interface

---

# Principle 14
Formal Semantics

Every institutional concept shall possess explicit semantic definition.

Implicit meaning is prohibited.

---

# Principle 15
Architectural Stability

Constitutional concepts evolve only through explicit governance.

Implementation may evolve independently.

---

# Governance Rule

The constitutional documents define the highest architectural authority of the Peculiar Library.

In case of conflict:

Constitution

↓

Architecture Principles

↓

Conceptual Model

↓

Semantic Metamodel

↓

Competency Questions

↓

Executive Intelligence Framework

↓

Ontology

↓

SHACL

↓

Taxonomy

↓

Implementation

The higher layer always prevails.
