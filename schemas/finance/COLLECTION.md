# Finance Collection Constitution

## Collection Name

Finance Collection

---

## Purpose

The Finance Collection exists to curate and govern the knowledge required for disciplined investing.

Its purpose is to organize concepts, relationships, evidence, and reasoning that support long-term investment decisions.

It does not predict markets.

It does not execute trades.

It governs the knowledge upon which investment decisions are made.

---

## Mission

To preserve, organize, validate, and expose financial knowledge in a form that is understandable by both humans and machines.

---

## Primary User

The Head Librarian.

Initially, the collection serves an active investor participating in the Nairobi Securities Exchange.

As the library grows, the collection will also support autonomous research agents, portfolio managers, analysts, and execution agents.

---

## Scope

The Finance Collection governs knowledge relating to:

- Securities
- Exchanges
- Portfolios
- Positions
- Orders
- Trades
- Cash
- Capital Allocation
- Investment Strategies
- Risk
- Valuation
- Dividends
- Corporate Actions
- Market Observations
- Investment Opportunities

---

## Out of Scope

The Finance Collection does not govern:

- Broker integrations
- Exchange connectivity
- User interfaces
- Machine learning models
- Price prediction
- Order execution infrastructure

Those belong to other collections or infrastructure.

---

## Collection Structure

Every Finance Collection shall contain:

- Ontology
- Taxonomy
- Shapes
- Queries
- Instances

No knowledge shall exist outside these governed artifacts.

---

## Guiding Principles

Meaning is defined by the ontology.

Classification is defined by the taxonomy.

Integrity is enforced through shapes.

Retrieval is performed through queries.

Facts are represented as instances.

Execution is performed by interpreters operating upon governed knowledge.

---

## Questions the Collection Must Answer

The collection shall enable answers to questions such as:

- What investment opportunities satisfy a defined investment policy?
- What risks exist within the current portfolio?
- Which securities satisfy specified investment criteria?
- What evidence supports an investment recommendation?
- What allocation changes preserve portfolio integrity?
- Which corporate actions affect owned securities?

---

## Governance

Every artifact within the Finance Collection shall be:

- Discoverable
- Versioned
- Verifiable
- Contract-compliant
- Semantically governed

Knowledge shall never depend upon hidden assumptions.

---

## Philosophy

The Finance Collection is not a trading system.

It is a curated body of executable financial knowledge.

Infrastructure serves the collection.

The collection serves the librarian.

The librarian serves knowledge.
