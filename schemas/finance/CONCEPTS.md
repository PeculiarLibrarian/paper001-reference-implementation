# Finance Collection Concept Catalogue

This document defines the controlled vocabulary of the Finance Collection.

These concepts form the semantic foundation from which ontologies,
taxonomies, shapes, queries, and instances are derived.

---

# Market

A financial environment where securities are exchanged.

---

# Exchange

An organized marketplace where securities are listed and traded.

Examples:

- Nairobi Securities Exchange

---

# Security

A financial instrument representing ownership or value.

---

# Equity

A security representing ownership in a company.

---

# Portfolio

A managed collection of investment positions.

---

# Position

Ownership of a security within a portfolio.

---

# Order

An instruction to buy or sell a security.

---

# Trade

The successful execution of an order.

---

# Cash

Available capital not currently invested.

---

# Capital Allocation

The distribution of capital across positions.

---

# Risk

Potential loss arising from uncertainty.

---

# Opportunity

A security or portfolio action satisfying defined investment criteria.

---

# Strategy

A repeatable investment philosophy used to evaluate opportunities.

Examples:

- Value Investing
- Dividend Investing
- Growth Investing
- Income Investing

---

# Valuation

Assessment of the intrinsic worth of a security.

---

# Dividend

Distribution of company earnings to shareholders.

---

# Corporate Action

An event initiated by a company affecting its securities.

Examples:

- Dividend
- Rights Issue
- Stock Split
- Bonus Issue

---

# Market Observation

A recorded fact describing the current market state.

---

# Investment Policy

A governed set of rules defining acceptable investment behaviour.

---

# Evidence

Information supporting an investment decision.

---

# Recommendation

A conclusion supported by governed evidence.
