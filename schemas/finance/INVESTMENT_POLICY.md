# Finance Collection Investment Policy

Purpose

The Finance Collection exists to preserve, organize, and reason over financial knowledge for long-term capital growth.

The collection is not designed for speculation.

It is designed for disciplined investment.

---

Primary Objective

Grow capital while preserving permanent purchasing power.

---

Investment Horizon

Long-term.

Years rather than days.

---

Primary Universe

Companies listed on the Nairobi Securities Exchange.

---

Evidence Priority

1. Audited Financial Statements

2. Annual Reports

3. Interim Reports

4. Dividend History

5. Corporate Actions

6. Regulatory Announcements

7. Market Observations

---

Decision Hierarchy

Knowledge

↓

Evidence

↓

Inference

↓

Recommendation

↓

Investment Decision

---

The library never reasons directly from price.

Price is evidence.

Knowledge is primary.

---

Collection Principle

Every recommendation must be explainable.

Every inference must be traceable.

Every observation must preserve provenance.
